IT and Equipment Policy

Equipment Provided:
All full-time employees receive a company laptop upon starting. Employees may
choose between a Mac or Windows laptop. Additional equipment such as monitors,
keyboards, and headsets can be requested through the IT portal.

Software Access:
Employees are granted access to standard company software (email, Slack, Google
Workspace) on their first day. Access to specialized tools requires manager
approval and is provisioned within 2 business days.

Password Policy:
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all company accounts.

VPN and Remote Access:
Employees working remotely must connect through the company VPN when accessing
internal systems or sensitive data. VPN credentials are provided during onboarding.

Equipment Return:
All company equipment must be returned within 5 business days of an employee's
last working day. Failure to return equipment may result in a deduction from
final pay, where permitted by local law.
